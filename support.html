How can we help?

WakeUpAlarm helps you manage your alarms and build a better morning routine with customizable wake-up missions. If you experience a problem or have a question, please contact us.

Frequently Asked Questions

My alarm is not working. What should I do?

Check your alarm settings, notification permissions and device settings. Make sure your iPhone is updated and that the alarm is correctly configured.

How do Wake-Up Missions work?

Wake-Up Missions require you to complete a selected task, such as solving a math problem or scanning a QR code or barcode, to complete your wake-up routine.

How can I report a technical problem?

Send us an email describing the issue, your iPhone model and your iOS version. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
